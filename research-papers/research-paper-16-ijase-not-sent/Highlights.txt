Multiplicative Information Gating: Decomposing News into Novelty, Materiality
and Polarity for Calibrated Selective Forecasting

Anandkumar Pardeshi and Sujata Deshmukh

- News is split into polarity, novelty and materiality, combined as a product.
- A veto bound proves the weakest of the three axes caps the event signal.
- Live ledger: nominal 90% price intervals covered only 70.7% of outcomes.
- A three-stage recalibration cut expected calibration error 0.35 to 0.05.
- A conviction gate abstaining on 94% of cases lifted the hit rate to 60.6%.
